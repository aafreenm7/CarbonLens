Academic Foundation & Syllabus Alignment

Demonstrates core Computer Engineering and Data Science curriculum principles.

Vectorized Data Processing

Utilizes Pandas series vectorization and NumPy operations for rapid calculations across activity columns without slow Python iteration loops.

Non-Destructive Cleaning Pipeline

Distinguishes between mathematically invalid records (e.g. negative hours) and statistical outliers (1.5 × IQR), providing a verifiable audit trail.

Scientific Integrity & Boundaries

Adheres to published carbon accounting standards (IEA, CEA India, Joule), strictly separating energy (\(\text{kWh}\)) from carbon (\(\text{kgCO}_2\text{e}\)) and avoiding double-counting.
