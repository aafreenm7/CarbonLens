Actionable Digital Sustainability Tips

Optimize Display Power

Reducing screen brightness from 100% to 70% can reduce display energy consumption by up to 20% on laptops. Set screen timeout to 5 minutes of inactivity.

Resolution & Streaming

Streaming in 1080p consumes significantly less transmission and CDN power than 4K streaming while offering near-identical clarity on standard screens.

Refine AI Prompts

Provide clear instructions and constraints in single prompts rather than submitting multiple exploratory questions to conserve inference compute.

Digital Clutter Abatement

Unsubscribe from automated newsletters and delete unused cloud backups to reduce persistent data center storage electricity demand.
